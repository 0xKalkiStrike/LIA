# ✨ LIA Feature Capabilities

LIA provides a comprehensive suite of AI capabilities across autonomous agency, productivity, media synthesis, and desktop automation.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 👤 3D Avatar & Neural Voice System
- **Interactive 3D VRM Avatar**: Real-time canvas rendering, emotion animations, lip-sync, and custom VRM upload support.
- **Piper Neural TTS**: Zero-latency offline speech synthesis with multi-accent support (US, UK, Indian English, Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali).
- **Custom Voice Pitch & Speed**: Real-time audio processing for customized tone, pitch, and speaking rate.

### 🤖 Multi-Agent Ecosystem
- **Coder Agent**: Generates, edits, and previews full web applications and code workspaces.
- **Presentation Agent**: Creates interactive HTML slide decks and downloadable `.pptx` presentations.
- **Image & Video Agents**: Generates images and video clips with AnimateDiff or Stable Diffusion pipelines.
- **Article & Research Agent**: Long-form article writing, deep research synthesis, and SEO publishing.
- **Scraper & Web Intelligence**: Surface and deep web research with Tor proxy privacy support and semantic vector caching.

### 📅 Personal Productivity Suite
- **Notes & Tasks**: Full CRUD management with per-user data isolation.
- **Calendar & Reminders**: Scheduled events and notification triggers.
- **Memory Vault**: Encrypted-like personal memory vault for storing sensitive user details, preferences, and facts.

### 💻 Desktop Automation & Vision
- **App Launching & Command Execution**: Direct launch of applications (Notepad, Calculator, PowerShell, Chrome, Edge) via natural language.
- **Vision & Telemetry**: Image explanation, OCR text extraction, gesture recognition (hand wave, smile, presence detection).
